# Fixed experiment for the additional views

After the ten training eggs had been recorded, and before any new validation views were captured, the experiment was fixed in `output/physical-multiangle-20261019/experiment-plan.json`. Its SHA-256 is `f42e42f8fb3757593bc5247196a2b94e22e9d51a3e5ea97acd3d3c2345b51bad`. The plan binds the active registry, original retained-camera manifest, and all ten new training capture records.

`python -m balut_ml.train_camera_angles` fits one three-class logistic head with C=100, the retained encoder, historical training features, and eligible views of the ten original training eggs. The original frame and three new frames share a total weight of 20 per physical egg. Class balancing counts each camera egg once. Validation and completed-test eggs cannot fit the head. Training refuses an incomplete collection, so capturing additional views cannot silently train a partial or mislabeled model.

The active margin, image-quality, and training-cosine thresholds remain unchanged. Training references are extended with eligible training views. The candidate is compared against the actual active model on the same ten new three-view validation groups, the original ten camera validation frames, and historical validation. The report counts physical eggs separately from photos, and uses the serving `view_consensus` rule for accepted group predictions. Raw group prediction is the winner of the mean of three raw score vectors; these scores are not probabilities.

The fixed comparison requires more correctly accepted new validation eggs without more wrongly accepted eggs, at least three correctly accepted eggs of each age, and no decrease in either age's raw or accepted correct counts. Historical raw correct, macro F1, and correct accepted counts cannot decrease, nor can wrongly accepted counts increase. Original camera validation correct accepted counts cannot decrease or wrongly accepted counts increase. All requirements must pass before `scripts/prepare_camera_trial.py --multiangle` will seal a new experimental release. That preparer verifies encoder and score parity on development images and the ten grouped decisions, then verifies saved software fixtures. It never opens original test images.

This is development evidence from the same physical collection. The original ten test eggs and their repeat photos remain excluded from fitting, selection, and new accuracy claims. Ninety additional photos do not become ninety independent eggs, and no 99% accuracy claim is supported. The active release is preserved unless an admitted candidate is explicitly prepared and deployed within the user's live-testing request.
