Eligibility and reference standard

DAY_16 means elapsed 384 to less than 408 incubation hours; DAY_18 means 432 to less than 456 hours. Record uncertainty in incubation timing. Exclude a candidate egg before prediction if its age cannot be assigned under this rule. Reject in the app means the source concept Unfertilized, not general vendor rejection. Its independent reference method must establish the intended label without consulting the model; a vendor statement alone is insufficient. A qualified assessor must define and document that method before collection. The collector checks that documentation exists; software does not prove its biological validity.

Sampling and repeat subset
The 90 template slots are a fixed shuffled order, random seed42. Register all identities before the first capture. Do not substitute eggs after observing errors. Slots01,11,21 of each class receive ten complete scans each, with these three eggs drawn from three different batches. The other81 eggs receive one complete scan. Total planned attempts=81+9*10=171; independent unique eggs=90. The first attempt of every egg is the primary accuracy unit. Repeats assess within-egg consistency and must never be counted as additional independent eggs.
