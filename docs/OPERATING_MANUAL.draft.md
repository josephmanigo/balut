# Draft operator manual

This procedure is for a guarded research prototype after hardware approval. It
is not authorization to energize the current source scaffold.

## Before each session

1. Confirm the chamber, removable tray, and silicone cups are clean, dry,
   correctly seated, and undamaged.
2. Confirm no wire, connector, or conductor is loose or exposed.
3. Release the emergency stop, close the empty lid, and power the box.
4. Wait for `READY / HANDA`. Any fault code must be acknowledged only after its
   cause is understood and corrected.
5. Select the documented incubation batch and physical-egg ID. Never estimate
   or edit the incubation start time to match a machine result.

## Load and scan

1. Power state must be ready and both motor/light indicators off.
2. Open the lid. Place the blunt and pointed ends gently in the silicone cups;
   do not force spring compression past the mechanical stop.
3. Close the lid and confirm egg presence and lid interlock.
4. Press the physical Start button or use an authenticated paired local client.
5. Do not open the lid during a scan except to test a controlled safety stop.
6. The display should progress from 1/8 through 8/8. The holder moves only with
   the candling light off; each picture is taken only after motion stops.

## Interpret the result

- `DAY 16-LIKE / KATULAD NG ARAW 16`: appearance resembles the validated Day 16
  profile. Read the separately recorded chronological age.
- `DAY 18-LIKE / KATULAD NG ARAW 18`: appearance resembles the validated Day 18
  profile. Read the separately recorded chronological age.
- `REJECT—REVIEW / HIWALAY AT SURIIN`: segregate for authorized human review.
  It is not confirmation of rottenness or food safety.
- `RECAPTURE / ULITIN ANG PAG-SCAN`: correct the shown cause and run a fresh
  scan. Do not relabel it as a decided egg.

The system never declares an egg safe, pathogen-free, sellable, or disposable.

## Safe-stop response

1. Do not simply reclose the lid and press Start repeatedly.
2. Confirm motor and candling light are off.
3. Read the fault code; inspect lid, E-stop, temperature, holder, home sensor,
   SD, camera, and egg position as directed.
4. Remove power before clearing a mechanical obstruction or opening the dry
   electronics area.
5. Record the fault. After the condition is removed, release E-stop, close the
   lid, acknowledge once, and start a new scan.

## Broken egg or spill

Use the approved hygiene and egg-disposition protocol. Isolate power, remove the
washable tray and cups without wetting electronics, clean and dry them, inspect
the chamber, and do not resume until the holder and interlocks pass inspection.

## End of session

Wait for all uploads or confirm the microSD queue is visible and retryable.
Power down with motor/light off. Clean removable contacts/tray and record any
fault, recapture, crack, unusual odor/appearance, or operator intervention.
