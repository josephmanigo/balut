# Three paused camera angles per egg

The user requested additional photos of the same 30 numbered eggs, then explicitly changed the capture request to multiple angles. The new local sequence pauses the identified spinner for each camera acquisition, resumes it, waits for at least 683 additional motor steps (approximately 120 degrees), and repeats until three distinct, checksum-valid fresh views are captured. The holder resumes before upload or inference. The 12-second firmware hold expiry and identity checks remain in effect, and the light stays on.

The user physically confirmed that the first numbered collection capture stopped for the camera and spun between captures. This supersedes the earlier report that the brief single-frame automatic pause was not observed. Motor step counts establish commanded spacing; they do not establish an exact physical angle because there is no position encoder.

`scripts/spinner_control.py` owns the shared three-view sequence. Camera acquisition failures resume rotation and retry only the affected view, up to five attempts. An incomplete group or duplicate frame is discarded. `scripts/camera_usb_bridge.py` consumes the camera's newline terminator before another request and retains CRC, size, JPEG, and fresh-buffer checks. USB corruption still occurs intermittently; retry success does not establish that the electrical or driver cause is fixed.

The Vercel connector sends one signed JSON group through `/api/v1/device/box/views` after all captures have finished and rotation has resumed. HMAC version 2 covers the path, event key, content type, timestamp, payload checksum, and body. The server accepts exactly three distinct camera frames only when its current camera session has a capture request less than 90 seconds old. This bounded capture window permits the motor/camera sequence without treating its normal preview gap as a disconnected camera. Other gaps retain the empty-view check. Three images are saved under one durable pending event and one prediction, and retries recover that event without duplicate records. The production body limit is 600 KB for the three base64-encoded frames; each decoded image remains limited to 128 KB.

The existing multi-view model rule is unchanged: at least two distinct views must pass their individual gates and agree, with no accepted conflicting label. The result records `captureProtocol: local-motor-three-views-v1` and identifies paused motor-step spacing. Uncertain or conflicting evidence still requests a rescan. This is not a new trained model or evidence of 99% accuracy.

Numbered collection uses `scripts/capture_camera_angles.py EGG-ID`, with the automatic connector stopped so only the collector owns the camera. Files and motor/capture metadata are stored under `output/physical-multiangle-20261019/EGG-ID/`. Existing captures cannot be overwritten. The collector imports completed groups using `balut_ml.camera_angles` into `data/local/retained-camera-angles-20261019/`. The original 2,091 manifest rows are preserved, all repeat views retain `physical-camera-20261018:EGG-ID` and the original partition, and each updated manifest gets an immutable snapshot. Its audit remains `COLLECTION_IN_PROGRESS` until all 30 eggs are captured.

The 90 requested new photos remain repeats of 30 physical eggs. The original training eggs are 1, 2, 3, 6, and 7 of each cohort; validation eggs are 4, 8, 9, 10, and 11; completed original test eggs are 5, 12, 13, 14, and 15. Repeat views of completed test eggs are stored but excluded from fitting and model selection. Model labels do not provide the dataset labels. Ages refer to the original user-confirmed cohorts, and capture timestamps are retained because exact ongoing incubation records have not been supplied.

Verification covers motor hold/release and step spacing, USB frame boundaries, signed route and payload validation, duplicate/partial/unrequested groups, a 25-second capture gap, durable one-event recovery, original-egg grouping, test exclusion, incomplete collection, and modified image rejection. The focused backend suites passed 100 tests; dataset import and retained-data regression suites passed nine. Physical acceptance of the deployed automatic three-angle flow and any accuracy gain require subsequent live testing and a separately reported grouped evaluation.
